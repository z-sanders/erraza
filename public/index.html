    Payment help

    Answers for your payment.

    Will I pay any fees?

    Review the final total before confirming your payment. Your bank, card provider or crypto network may charge additional fees.

    How do I know my payment is complete?

    This page shows “Payment received” once your payment is confirmed complete. Until then, use “Check status” to see the latest update.

    My payment is pending or failed. What should I do?

    If you sent funds or see a card charge, don’t pay again. Check the status, then contact support with the order reference if you need help.

    Can I close this page?

    Yes. Come back in the same browser and choose “Return to payment”. Closing the page does not cancel a payment.

    Need help? Include your order reference when you contact support.

    Your privacy

    This browser remembers your payment so you can return and check its status. If a request is interrupted, it temporarily saves the details needed to avoid starting it twice.

    Card and identity details are entered with the payment provider, not on this page. The provider’s privacy policy applies there.

    This checkout does not use third-party tracking scripts.
